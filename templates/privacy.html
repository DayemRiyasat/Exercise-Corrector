Privacy

What is stored, and what never is

Short version: camera frames and videos are analysed and then discarded, never stored, and your history is tied to an anonymous cookie you can clear at any time.

Analysed on the server, then discarded
  • Webcam frames are sent to the server, analysed in memory and discarded straight away
  • An uploaded video is held in a temporary file while it is analysed, then deleted
  • An uploaded photo is analysed in memory and discarded

Every exercise uses a detailed pose model that runs on the server. No image or video is kept after the analysis, and none is ever written to your history.

What is sent to the server
  • The camera frames, video or photo being analysed (see above)
  • Which exercise you selected
  • When you finish a set: its duration, rep or window count, clean count and fault tally

Frames are used for the analysis only and are not stored.

How you are identified

You are not asked for a name, an email or a password. On your first visit the server sets a cookie containing a random ID, and your saved sessions are attached to that ID. It identifies a browser, not a person.

Clearing your cookies, or using a private window, gives you a clean slate. Deleting your history from the dashboard removes the stored rows entirely.

Delete everything

Clear history on the dashboard wipes every session stored against your browser's ID.
