Privacy

What is stored, and what never is

Short version: your video stays on your device, and your history is tied to an anonymous cookie you can clear at any time.

What never leaves your device
  • Your webcam stream
  • Any video file you upload for analysis
  • Any photo you upload for analysis

Pose detection runs in your browser. Files you select are read locally and are never transmitted.

What is sent to the server
  • {{ site_stats.landmarks }} joint coordinates per analysed frame, as plain numbers
  • Which exercise you selected
  • When you finish a set: its duration, rep count, clean-rep count and fault tally

Coordinates are used to run the classifier for that frame and are not written to disk.

How you are identified

You are not asked for a name, an email or a password. On your first visit the server sets a cookie containing a random ID, and your saved sessions are attached to that ID. It identifies a browser, not a person.

Clearing your cookies, or using a private window, gives you a clean slate. Deleting your history from the dashboard removes the stored rows entirely.

Delete everything

Clear history on the dashboard wipes every session stored against your browser's ID.
